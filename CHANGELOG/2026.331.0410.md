# 2026.331.0410 — feat(box): the qwen3-tts image, its bed, and the bed's lazy-shim contract

## Summary

Move the `qwen3-tts` box and its `check-qwen3-tts-pod` bed out of `distro-cachyos` into the repo that
owns the service (`AGENTS.md` "Repo classes and naming": a bed lives with the thing it tests; a
`distro-*` repo holds the distro family, not one service's box).

**The box** composes the LOCAL `qwen3-tts` candy (the box proves this tree, not a pinned release of
itself), and the project header declares what the distro copy inherited: `base: cachyos.cachyos` with
the CachyOS base imported at the project root, and `defaults.builder.{pixi,npm,cargo,aur}:
arch.arch-builder` with `arch` imported. Torch's cu130 wheels carry their own CUDA runtime and the
driver arrives through CDI, so no CUDA layer is composed.

**Moving the bed exposed TWO real defects, both fixed where they belong:**

1. **The bed asserted the wrong shim contract.** It demanded `/health` 200 within 5 minutes — the
   *eager-loading* shim's contract. This repo's shim spawns the worker on the first synthesis and
   kills it after the idle TTL, so at rest `/health` answers **503 with `status":"unloaded"`**
   (measured: 503 for the whole 5-minute window, twice). The bed now asserts the real contract in
   both halves — **unloaded at rest** (503, `"status":"unloaded"`, `"ready":false`) and **ready once
   a request started the worker** (200, `"status":"ok"`) — the old claim deleted, not relaxed. This
   is exactly the drift a bed in another repo cannot avoid: it was pinned to a release of this candy
   older than the one it tests.
2. **A keep-id GPU unit could not find its driver.** The bed's first run died at synthesis with
   `libcuda.so cannot found!`; the RCA (measured in the container) is that the derived devices-only
   spec drops the toolkit hooks that refresh `/etc/ld.so.cache`, so a cache-reading consumer
   (`triton`'s `libcuda_dirs`) cannot see the bound library. Fixed in
   **`opencharly/plugin-deploy-pod#104`** (merged, tag `v2026.331.0349`) — not worked around here —
   with the contract statement in **`opencharly/layer-cdi#28`**.

It also adds the canonical `.gitignore` entries the build needs (`.containerignore`,
`.dockerignore`, `.check/`, …), **keeping this repo's four existing entries in place**.
