# 2026.328.1555 — feat: load Qwen3-TTS on demand, gate on free VRAM, release all VRAM when idle

## Summary

Turn the Qwen3-TTS service into an **on-demand** one: nothing is loaded at start, a synthesis
request waits (bounded) for free VRAM and starts a worker, and after 10 idle minutes the worker is
**killed** so its weights *and* CUDA context are released.

- `qwen3-tts-server.py` becomes a torch-free public shim: request validation, the bounded
  free-VRAM gate (`QWEN3_TTS_GPU_MIN_FREE_MIB` 4096 / `QWEN3_TTS_GPU_WAIT_SECONDS` 600), the
  worker lifecycle (spawn, pipe readiness, idle kill), and a UDS proxy. It imports neither torch
  nor `qwen_tts`.
- `qwen3-tts-worker.py` (new) owns torch + `qwen-tts`: loads the checkpoint in its lifespan,
  writes the ready pipe, serves `/synth` and `/voices` over a Unix socket, and arms
  `PR_SET_PDEATHSIG` so a dead shim cannot orphan a GPU holder.
- `qwen3_tts_common.py` (new) holds one copy of the encoding probe, voice list, encoder AND the
  error helpers (round 2: `error_summary` / `error_response` moved here, R2).
- `QWEN3_TTS_IDLE_UNLOAD_S` now defaults to **600** (was 0); every knob is an env var.
- The old in-process idle unload and `tests/test_idle_unload.py` are deleted; three baked tests
  replace them and the two 300-line inline checks, plus the real-worker CPU test.

`/health` keeps its readiness meaning (200 only when a synthesis can be served) and gains the
`unloaded` / `waiting_for_vram` / `unloading` states. The wire contract (`/v1/audio/speech`,
`/v1/audio/voices`, `/v1/models`) is unchanged.

**Non-blocking notes, dispositioned:** the unused `_read_ready_pipe(started)` parameter is removed.
The idle-kill path cannot truncate an in-flight response: the shim increments `_active` under
`_active_lock` before `ensure_worker`, and the idle loop takes the same lock, asserts `_active == 0`
and only then marks `stopping`; a request that arrives after that waits on the phase and starts a
fresh worker. A hard SIGKILL of the shim (container stop) does terminate the worker via
PR_SET_PDEATHSIG, which can drop that one response — that is a hard stop, not the idle path.
